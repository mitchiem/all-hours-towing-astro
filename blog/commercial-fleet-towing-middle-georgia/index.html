What Constitutes Fleet Towing

Fleet towing covers light to medium-duty commercial vehicles: cargo vans, box trucks up to 26 feet, utility trucks, pickup trucks with service bodies, and passenger vans. Our medium-duty flatbeds can handle vehicles up to 10,000 pounds, covering most Class 1-3 commercial fleets. For heavier Class 4-6 trucks, we coordinate with our heavy-duty partners while providing on-site assessment and traffic control.
