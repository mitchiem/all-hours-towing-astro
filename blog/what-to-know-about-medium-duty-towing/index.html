What to Know About Medium Duty Towing

Defining Medium Duty Towing

Vehicle classes and towing capacities can be confusing. When you search for “medium duty towing in Macon,” you’re likely dealing with a vehicle larger than a standard sedan but not a full semi-truck. Medium duty typically covers vehicles in the 10,001 to 26,000-pound Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR) range. This includes box trucks, commercial vans, light buses, some RVs, and large pickup trucks hauling heavy loads. All Hours Towing Macon GA operates medium duty flatbeds that safely handle these heavier vehicles on Middle Georgia roads.

Vehicles That Require Medium Duty Towing

  • Isuzu NPR and Ford F-550 Box Trucks: Common for local deliveries around Macon’s business districts and the industrial parks off Broadway.
  • Passenger and Shuttle Vans: Church vans from congregations like Ingleside Baptist, or hotel shuttles ferrying guests to the Macon Marriott.
  • Class C RVs and Travel Trailers: Winter snowbirds passing through on I-75 often need a medium duty tow when their rig breaks down near the Byron exit.
  • Utility Trucks: Bucket trucks and service body F-550s used by Georgia Power crews and cable contractors.

Why Medium Duty Towing Requires Expertise

These vehicles present unique challenges. Their center of gravity is often higher, especially with box trucks and RVs, making them prone to sway during towing. Braking distances are longer, requiring drivers experienced in weight distribution and momentum management. In Macon, older streets like those near the historic district have tight turns and low-hanging branches that smaller wreckers negotiate easily but which can snag tall vehicles.

Our Equipment
