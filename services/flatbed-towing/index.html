Professional Flatbed Towing You Can Trust

At All Hours Towing, we pride ourselves on providing professional flatbed towing. Our trained technicians use state-of-the-art equipment to ensure the job is done right the first time.

Whether you're dealing with a breakdown on I-75 near Macon, locked out of your vehicle in Warner Robins, or need urgent assistance anywhere in our service area, our team is ready to help. We understand that vehicle emergencies are stressful, which is why we prioritize fast response times and transparent communication.

Flatbed towing is the gold standard for vehicle transport, and at All Hours Towing Macon GA, we operate a fleet of modern flatbed trucks ready to protect your vehicle during transport across Macon, Warner Robins, Byron, and all of Middle Georgia. Whether you drive an all-wheel-drive Subaru, a low-slung Corvette, a lifted Jeep, or a luxury Mercedes-Benz, our flatbed service ensures all four wheels stay off the ground—eliminating the risk of drivetrain, transmission, and tire damage that can occur with traditional wheel-lift towing.Why does flatbed towing matter specifically in Macon? The city's mix of historic neighborhoods, modern developments, and major highways creates unique towing challenges. In downtown Macon's older districts, narrow streets and tight parking garages demand a flatbed operator who can position the truck precisely without blocking traffic or scraping adjacent vehicles. On I-75 and I-475, where speeds are high and shoulders are narrow, flatbed loading is faster and safer than wheel-lift hookups because the entire vehicle is winched onto the bed from the rear, keeping the operator and your car out of the travel lanes. In rural areas around Lizella and Payne City, where dirt roads and soft shoulders are common, a flatbed truck's low-angle ramp prevents the scraping and bottoming out that can occur with traditional tow trucks.Our flatbed operators handle every vehicle as if it were their own. We use soft straps—never chains—to secure your car to the bed, protecting the suspension components and undercarriage. For vehicles with exceptionally low ground clearance, we use approach ramps and wooden blocks to create a gentler loading angle. For AWD and 4WD vehicles, we confirm that all four wheels are properly secured and that the vehicle is transported in neutral without any wheels rotating—critical for protecting the transfer case and differentials.Beyond everyday transport, our flatbed trucks serve Macon's automotive community in specialized ways. We work with local dealerships like Butler Toyota, Five Star Chevrolet, and Hutchinson Kia to transport new and used inventory between lots. We assist Macon's collision repair shops by delivering damaged vehicles directly to body shop bays. We help classic car collectors move vintage vehicles to and from shows like the annual Cherry Blossom Festival car exhibition. And for residents relocating within Georgia or to neighboring states, our flatbed service provides a safe, insured long-distance transport option. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is flatbed towing safer for my vehicle?

Flatbed towing keeps all four wheels off the ground, eliminating wear on your vehicle's drivetrain, transmission, and tires. It's the recommended method for AWD vehicles, luxury cars, sports cars, and any vehicle with low ground clearance.

Q: Do you offer flatbed towing in Warner Robins and Byron?

Yes, our flatbed trucks serve the entire Middle Georgia area including Warner Robins and Byron. We can reach you quickly and transport your vehicle safely to any destination.

Q: Can a flatbed tow truck access tight spaces in Macon?

Our flatbed operators are experienced in navigating Macon's residential streets, parking decks, and tight commercial areas. If access is limited, we'll assess the situation and find the safest approach for your vehicle.
