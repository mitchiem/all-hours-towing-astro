Professional EV Towing Guide You Can Trust

At All Hours Towing, we pride ourselves on providing professional ev towing guide. Our trained technicians use state-of-the-art equipment to ensure the job is done right the first time.

Whether you're dealing with a breakdown on I-75 near Macon, locked out of your vehicle in Warner Robins, or need urgent assistance anywhere in our service area, our team is ready to help. We understand that vehicle emergencies are stressful, which is why we prioritize fast response times and transparent communication.

Electric vehicles represent the fastest-growing segment of vehicles on Macon's roads, but they require specialized towing knowledge that many traditional towing companies lack. All Hours Towing Macon GA is trained and equipped for safe EV towing, using flatbed trucks exclusively for electric vehicle transport. Most EVs—including all Tesla models, Ford Mustang Mach-E, Hyundai Ioniq 5/6, Kia EV6, Chevrolet Bolt, Nissan Leaf, and Rivian trucks—cannot have any wheels on the ground during towing without risking severe damage to their electric motors, regenerative braking systems, and high-voltage battery packs. Our flatbed trucks keep all four wheels elevated, eliminating any risk to the drivetrain. We serve EV owners throughout Macon, Warner Robins, and Byron with the same 24/7 availability and rapid response times as our conventional towing services. Our operators understand the additional procedures required for EVs: how to put a Tesla into tow mode, how to locate the manual door releases on vehicles with electronic door handles (critical during lockouts), and how to safely winch an EV onto a flatbed without damaging the battery enclosure or underbody aero panels. We also understand that EV drivers in Macon may need towing to specific destinations: the Tesla Supercharger station on Tom Hill Sr. Blvd, the Electrify America stations at the Macon Mall, or the growing network of ChargePoint and EVgo chargers around Middle Georgia. If your EV runs out of charge on I-75 between Macon and Atlanta, we can tow you directly to the nearest compatible charger rather than a random repair shop. For EV owners with home charging setups, we can deliver your vehicle directly to your driveway and even help navigate tight garage clearances. As Macon's newest car dealerships bring more EVs to Middle Georgia, All Hours Towing is your local EV towing specialist.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can all EVs be towed with a regular tow truck?

No—most EVs must be transported on a flatbed with all four wheels off the ground. Towing an EV with wheels on the road can severely damage the electric motors and battery. All Hours Towing uses flatbed trucks for all EV towing.

Q: Do you tow Teslas in Macon?

Yes, we tow all Tesla models (Model S, 3, X, Y, Cybertruck) throughout Macon, Warner Robins, and Byron. We know how to put Teslas into tow mode and use flatbed transport for safe delivery.

Q: What if my EV runs out of charge on I-75?

We'll dispatch a flatbed truck to transport your EV to the nearest compatible charging station—whether that's a Supercharger, Electrify America, or other network in the Macon area.
