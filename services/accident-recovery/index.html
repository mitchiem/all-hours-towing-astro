Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What should I do immediately after an accident?

First, check yourself and passengers for injuries—call 911 if anyone is hurt. If it's safe and vehicles are drivable, move to the shoulder and turn on hazard lights. Then call All Hours Towing at (478) 500-8118. Our team coordinates with law enforcement and handles vehicle recovery while you focus on your wellbeing.

Q: Can you recover my vehicle from any location?

Yes, we recover vehicles from ditches, embankments, medians, guardrails, and any off-road location. Our winching equipment and trained technicians can safely extract vehicles without causing additional damage. We serve Macon, Warner Robins, Warner Robins, and throughout Middle Georgia.

Q: Do you work with insurance companies for accident recovery?

Yes, we work with all major insurance carriers. We provide detailed invoices, scene photographs, and documentation suitable for insurance claims. We can bill your insurance directly in many cases and help guide you through the claims process.

Q: How quickly can you arrive at an accident scene?

Our average response time to accident scenes is 20-30 minutes across our service area. We have two locations—Macon and Warner Robins—staged strategically near major highways including I-75, I-16, I-20, and I-475 for rapid deployment.

Q: What equipment do you use for accident recovery?

We deploy flatbed trucks for damaged vehicles, heavy winches for off-road recoveries, and scene safety equipment including cones, warning triangles, and spill containment materials. Our technicians are trained in accident scene management and carry appropriate PPE.

Q: Can you tow my vehicle to my preferred repair shop?

Absolutely. You have the right to choose where your vehicle is taken. Tell our driver your preferred repair shop, dealership, or storage location. If you're unsure, we can recommend trusted local shops that work with all major insurance carriers.
