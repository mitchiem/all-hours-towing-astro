Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How quickly can you arrive for roadside assistance in Macon?

Our average response time for roadside assistance in the Macon area is 20-30 minutes. We have two strategic locations in Macon and Warner Robins to ensure fast deployment across our service area.

Q: What roadside assistance services do you offer?

We offer jumpstarts for dead batteries, lockout services (get back in your car without damage), flat tire changes, fuel delivery (if you run out of gas), and vehicle extraction from ditch or mud situations.

Q: Do you offer roadside assistance for commercial vehicles?

Yes! We provide roadside assistance for both personal and commercial vehicles, including semi-trucks, delivery vans, and fleet vehicles. Our heavy-duty equipment can handle vehicles of all sizes.

Q: Are your technicians certified for roadside assistance?

All our roadside assistance technicians are fully trained, insured, and experienced. We use professional-grade tools and follow industry best practices to ensure safe, damage-free service.

Q: Can you help if I'm stranded on I-75 or I-20?

Absolutely. We frequently assist stranded motorists on I-75, I-16, I-20, and other major highways in Middle Georgia and South Atlanta. We work closely with local law enforcement and can often reach you faster than waiting for a police-initiated tow.

Q: What should I do while waiting for roadside assistance?

Move your vehicle to a safe location if possible (off the road, away from traffic). Turn on your hazard lights. If you're on a highway, stay inside your vehicle with seatbelt on. Have your location ready to share with our dispatcher.

Q: Do you service all vehicle types for roadside assistance?

We service cars, trucks, SUVs, motorcycles, RVs, and most commercial vehicles. If you have a unique vehicle or situation, call us and we'll do our best to help.
