Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What vehicles qualify for light duty towing?

Light duty towing covers most passenger vehicles including cars, SUVs, crossovers, and small pickup trucks. Medium duty covers larger trucks, cargo vans, and vehicles up to 26,000 lbs.

Q: Do you use flatbed tow trucks?

Yes, we primarily use flatbed tow trucks which are the safest method for modern vehicles. Flatbed towing eliminates the risk of damage to the vehicle's drivetrain that can occur with traditional tow dollies.

Q: Can you tow my car from a parking garage or tight space?

Our fleet includes compact tow trucks specifically designed for tight spaces, parking garages, and areas with limited access. Describe your situation when you call and we'll send appropriate equipment.

Q: Do you offer long-distance towing services?

Yes, we provide long-distance towing throughout Georgia and can arrange transport to surrounding states. Our fleet and partnerships allow us to handle moves of any distance efficiently.

Q: What areas do you serve for light duty towing?

We serve Macon, Warner Robins, Milledgeville, Warner Robins, Conyers, Monroe, Perry, Gray, and all surrounding Middle Georgia and South Atlanta communities.
